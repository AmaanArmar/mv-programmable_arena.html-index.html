How to Play

1. Programming Phase — Each player (hot-seat) builds a queue of up to 10 actions.
• Click a direction (Forward / Back / Left / Right) — uses the Steps value
• Set shots (1–5) and press + Shoot
• Press Done → Next when finished. Next player programs.

2. Run Round — After everyone is done, press ▶ RUN ROUND. All bots execute their queues simultaneously and smoothly.
Bots face the direction they move. Obstacles block movement and bullets.
Damage = points. Last bot(s) standing or highest score after rounds wins.

Grid: 10×10 • Obstacles: grey blocks • Facing shown by the white nose.
